GALLERY ARCANA — PRIVATE COMMERCE ENGINE v0.5

Implemented on the existing v0.4 backend, retaining its marketplace, encrypted
legacy delivery, disputes, durable mock payments and recovery protections.

NEW FUNCTIONALITY
- Versioned browser-compatible client cryptography package; local chunked
  AES-GCM capsules, encrypted metadata, pinned RFC 9180 HPKE buyer envelopes.
- Encryption-key possession challenges, signed rotations, revision-ordered
  history, encrypted offline recovery backups and explicit continuity resets.
- Participant-only encrypted conversations and bounded attachments; financial
  amounts/state remain backend-verifiable operator-visible metadata.
- Prepositioned buyer keys before checkout; funding-gated authorized delivery,
  local decryption, buyer acceptance and durable payout authorization.
- One-time private invitations: hashed body-only capability, designated buyers,
  fixed/negotiated price, expiry/revocation, atomic redemption.
- Trade Passport v2: minimal consent-based facts, threshold counts, audience/
  holder proofs, expiry/revocation, signing-key history/compromise handling.
- Consent-based reviewer-only encrypted evidence and append-only application
  decision records, preserving conflicting-release/refund protections.
- Independent DNS/public-GitHub/EIP-191 point-in-time control challenges; no
  legal ownership guarantee or external custody/transfer.

storage protection. Static keys have no Signal-like forward secrecy. Browser
interoperability, independent crypto review, production key/custody/backup and

NFT/domain/repository transfer or cross-chain custody is implemented.
